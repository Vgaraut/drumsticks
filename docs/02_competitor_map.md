# 02 — Competitor Map

## Important competitors / adjacent products

### Drum Notes / Drumap

Type: mobile drum beat and notation app.

Signals:

- public app listing claims 200k+ drummers/percussionists;
- 400k+ scores;
- drum notation and sharing.

Implication:

- real demand exists;
- mobile-first notation is a proven direction;
- opportunity is a better web/desktop workflow, not “first drum notation app ever.”

### Groove Scribe

Type: browser-based drum groove creator.

Signals:

- grid/notation-like workflow;
- listen/share/print/download flows;
- simple and accessible.

Implication:

- the web can work for short drum patterns;
- simplicity matters;
- opportunity is deeper project workflow, kit customization, better playback/export, AI patching.

### Aered

Type: drum-specific notation editor.

Signals:

- designed specifically for drummers;
- focuses on placing drum notes quickly;
- supports sheet creation workflows.

Implication:

- drummer-specific notation UX is a recognized category;
- opportunity is a modern standalone web app with grid-first writing.

### Guitar Pro

Type: broad notation/tab product with drum support.

Signals:

- playback;
- MIDI/PDF/export workflows;
- mature general-purpose product.

Implication:

- do not compete as a universal notation app;
- compete on speed, drummer-specific UX, kit-first workflow, and AI patching.

### Soundslice

Type: notation/practice platform.

Signals:

- supports percussion notation;
- custom percussion maps;
- playback and practice workflows.

Implication:

- percussion maps and playback are important;
- opportunity is a simpler creation workspace for drummers.

## Positioning

DrumForge should not claim:

> “the only drum notation software.”

Better positioning:

> “A fast web workspace for writing drum parts: build your kit, click grooves, hear them instantly, export MIDI/PDF, and use AI to refine selected bars.”

## Differentiation matrix

### Against notation suites

- faster for drum grooves;
- less notation setup;
- no heavy learning curve;
- grid-first workflow;
- AI patch assist.

### Against DAWs

- not overloaded with production features;
- easier printable output;
- kit and notation are first-class;
- better for practice/teaching.

### Against simple groove tools

- projects, not just snippets;
- custom kit;
- MIDI/PDF export as first-class workflows;
- AI transformations;
- future cloud sharing.

## Strategic warning

If DrumForge becomes a bad DAW, it loses.

If DrumForge becomes a lightweight “drum notebook with sound,” it can win a focused niche.
